About Me

I'm Reihaneh, a 25-year-old digital illustrator specialising in Procreate. Creating colored pencil paintings—and later, digital drawings—was my summer hobby from middle school until my undergraduate years in law school. It wasn’t until my gap year in 2024 that I began producing original digital artworks, which are inspired by the technological challenges I read about and simple moments in life I appreciate. I'm still early in this stage, and as I find my way through art, I continue my studies in technology law, delving deeper into how law, creativity, and technology weave together.
